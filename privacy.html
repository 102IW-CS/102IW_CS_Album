The short version

  • We collect only what the album needs: your name, email, the photos you add, and the choices you make about sharing them.
  • Photos are private until you share them. Admins can see all photos so they can run the site safely.
  • No ads, no tracking, no selling, no AI training. The site uses no analytics or advertising cookies.
  • Everything is stored by Supabase in the United States, encrypted in transit and at rest.
  • You can download your photos at any time, and ask us to delete your account and everything in it.

Who this applies to

This policy covers the Photo Share album (the album page, this page, the Terms, and the contact page). The site is run by Mohan Karki (“we”, “us”), who decides how information on it is used. It is a personal, non-commercial site, not the website of any employer or organization. It works alongside our Terms of Service.

You have the rights described in section 08, and may complain to your local data protection authority.
  • Visitors outside the United States: Your information is stored and processed in the United States, which may have different data protection rules from where you live.

02Photo details and location data

Photos taken on phones and cameras often contain hidden details, such as the date, camera model, and sometimes the exact location where the photo was taken.

  • With “Optimize” on (the default), the site re-saves each photo before uploading it, on your device. This removes location data and other hidden details.
  • With “Optimize” off, the original file is uploaded unchanged, including any location data. Anyone who can see the photo can download that original file.
  • Preview images never contain hidden details. GIFs are always uploaded unchanged.

Tip: Leave Optimize on unless you need the full original file. If you turn it off, check that you're comfortable sharing where each photo was taken.

06How long we keep things

  • Photos and albums: until you or an admin delete them. Deleting a photo removes the file and its preview from storage straight away.
  • Your account: until you ask us to delete it, or an admin removes it. Deleting an account removes your sign-in, member record, shares and removal requests. Your photos are deleted too, unless you ask for them to stay for the people you shared them with.
  • Invite links: expire after 30 days, or sooner if turned off.
  • Contact messages: until an admin deletes them. Admins can set read messages to delete automatically after 30 days to 1 year. Unread messages are never deleted automatically.
  • Backups and logs: our providers may keep backup copies and logs for a limited time under their own policies, after which they're deleted.
  • Copies others made: photos people downloaded while you shared them with them are outside our control.

07Where it's stored, and security

  • Your account, database records and photos are stored by Supabase in the United States (Amazon Web Services, US East region).
  • All connections use HTTPS (TLS). Supabase encrypts stored data at rest.
  • Photos are kept in a private storage area. Each one can only be opened through a link that expires after a short time, and only by someone allowed to see it.
  • Database access rules check every request, so members can only reach what they're allowed to.
  • New accounts see nothing until approved, and sessions can end automatically after inactivity.

No system is perfectly secure. If we learn of a breach that affects your information, we'll tell the people affected without unnecessary delay, and explain what happened and what to do.

08Your choices and rights

  • Download your photos at any time, one by one, several at once, or all of yours from My account as a ZIP file.
  • Delete your own photos and albums yourself, and stop sharing or turn off invite links whenever you like.
  • Change your name and password yourself in My account. Ask us for a copy of anything else we hold about you, or to correct it.
  • Delete your account and everything in it yourself, under My account, or ask us to do it (see section 06).
  • Object or ask us to limit how we use your information, where the law gives you that right.
  • Emails: we only send emails you need for your account, such as password resets. There's no newsletter or marketing to opt out of.

To make a request, email from the address on your account. We'll reply within 30 days, and we won't treat you differently for asking.

09Children

The site isn't meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly let them create accounts. If we learn that a child under 13 has an account, we'll delete it. Members aged 13 to 17 need a parent's or guardian's permission.

Photos of children are often what families want to share. Please share them only with people you trust, and only with the consent of the child's parent or guardian. Photos of a child are removed on request from their parent or guardian.
