The short version

  • Your photos stay yours. We only store and show them so the album works. We never sell them, advertise with them, or use them to train AI.
  • Everything starts private. You choose who sees each album or photo. Admins can see everything, so they can keep the album safe.
  • Only post what you have the right to share, and respect the people in your photos, especially children.
  • It's a free, personal site. Keep your own copies of anything important.

05Sharing and what others can do

  • Private by default. New albums and photos are visible only to you and the admins until you share them.
  • You choose the audience. You can share with every member, with members you pick, or through an invite link that works for 30 days. Anyone who opens an invite link still needs an approved account to see anything. You can stop sharing or turn off a link at any time.
  • People you share with can download. Once someone has downloaded or screenshotted a photo, that copy is outside the site's control. Only share what you're comfortable with them keeping.
  • Shared albums are collaborative. People who can see an album can add their own photos to it. They keep ownership of the photos they add, and only they (or an admin) can delete them.
  • Asking for something to be taken down. If you appear in a photo, or don't want something shown, use Request removal. The person who uploaded it, or an admin, decides. We ask everyone to honor reasonable requests, especially about photos of themselves or their children.

06Content rules

This is a place for friends and colleagues to share memories. To keep it that way, you agree not to upload, share or do any of the following:

Respect the people in your photos

  • Photos of people taken in private settings (for example at home, in changing rooms or bathrooms), or that they'd reasonably expect not to be shared, without their permission.
  • Photos of children unless you are their parent or guardian or have that person's permission. Keep photos of children in albums you share only with people you trust.
  • Content that harasses, bullies, threatens, demeans or embarrasses someone, or that reveals other people's private information, such as ID cards, documents, addresses or phone numbers.

Keep it lawful and appropriate

  • Nudity, sexual content or graphic violence.
  • Any sexual content involving a minor. It will be removed, the account closed, and it will be reported to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children and law enforcement.
  • Content that is illegal, hateful, or that promotes violence or self-harm.
  • Content you don't have the rights to, such as other people's photography, artwork or copyrighted material.

Protect sensitive work information

  • Classified or controlled information, or anything your employer or organization doesn't allow to be photographed or shared. That includes badges and ID cards, access codes, screens, documents, and secure or restricted areas.
  • Follow your organization's photography and operations-security rules. When in doubt, leave it out.

Don't misuse the site

  • Trying to get around the access rules, see content not shared with you, or break, overload or probe the site's security.
  • Using bots or scripts to copy content in bulk, uploading malware, or sending spam, ads or other commercial messages through the site or its contact form.
  • Sharing your account, or helping someone who has been removed get back in.
